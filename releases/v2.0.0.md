# Spanda 2.0.0 | Sangam

*Where Sound, Color, and Motion Meet.*

In Sanskrit, **Sangam (संगम)** means confluence, the coming together of different elements. Reflecting this idea, Spanda now unites music, visuals, and control in a more seamless experience, with new audio-reactive scenes, Spotify integration, recording capabilities, and a redesigned interface.

## What's Inside

- **Four New Visual Experiences:** Explore Particle Constellation, Liquid Interference, Typography, and Kaleidoscope, expanding Spanda's collection to eight unique audio-reactive scenes.
- **Spotify Integration:** View album artwork, track information, and control playback, skipping, and seeking directly from Spanda, without OAuth or Spicetify.
- **Record Your Visuals:** Capture your visual experiences with system audio in WebM format, supporting up to 720p at 30 FPS for five minutes.
- **Automatic Scene Rotation:** Switch between your favorite scenes at customizable intervals with smooth crossfade transitions.
- **Redesigned Glass HUD:** A refreshed interface bringing visualization controls, Spotify integration, and diagnostics together with responsive tabs and smoother interactions.
- **Enhanced Audio Diagnostics:** Improved spectrum analysis, waveforms, stereo meters, and dBFS readings for better insight into your audio.
- **Refined Experience:** Improved fullscreen behavior, keyboard controls, persistent visual preferences, and convenient sensitivity reset.

## Getting Started

Download **`Spanda_2.0.0_x64-setup.exe`**, close any running instance of Spanda, and run the installer to experience Sangam.

**Platform:** Windows (x64)

**Current limitations:** Audio capture supports Windows system playback only. Microphone input and macOS/Linux capture are not yet supported. Spotify controls require the Windows desktop application. Recording is experimental, with MP4 export and broader synchronization testing planned for future releases.
